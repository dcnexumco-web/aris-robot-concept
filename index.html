01

CONCEPT

NOT JUST A
HOUSE ASSISTANT.

ARIS is designed around a simple idea: a residential robot should not only respond to commands, but understand its environment.

The system combines household assistance with residential security. It is intended to observe its surroundings, identify objects and people, detect unusual situations and determine an appropriate response in selected emergency scenarios.
